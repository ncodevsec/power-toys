# Power Toys — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Power Toys is a browser extension for security testing and bug hunting. **All processing happens locally in your browser.** Power Toys has no server, no account, no analytics, and no telemetry. The developer never receives your data.

## What the extension accesses
- **The current page** (links, inline scripts, comments, storage, cookies, response headers) — only when you open the popup or use the context menu, and only to show you results.
- **Selected text** — only when you pick a Power Toys context-menu action.
- **Requests to the site you are inspecting** — the Paths and Headers tools send requests to the site you are viewing (never to any other server).

## What is stored
Settings, custom patterns, scan results and recent job state are stored in `chrome.storage` on your device (and in Chrome sync storage for settings, if you have Chrome Sync enabled). You can clear them at any time by removing the extension.

## What is shared
Nothing. No data is sold, transferred, or sent to third parties.
